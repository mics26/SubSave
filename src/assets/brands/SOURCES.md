# Bundled subscription icons

- `spotify.svg`, `netflix.svg`, and `github.svg`: [Simple Icons v14.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/14.0.0/icons), under CC0-1.0.
  The downloaded SVG paths are unchanged; the root fill uses the brand color.
  See the accompanying `LICENSE.md`.
- `google-one.svg`: [Google's Google One asset](https://ssl.gstatic.com/subscriptions/management/home/logo_one_48dp_1147f17d6d2f67ab3a9ce3c55600631b.svg), unchanged.

Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. These assets identify
subscriptions in the tracker; they do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
All four files are bundled locally; no icon CDN requests are made at runtime.
